While editing, click an answer and choose Full marks, Partial marks or Not allowed. The marking changes to give it those marks.

Partial marks gives half. The marking gets an Accepted Answers step with that answer, showing “50% of the marks”. To give another share, open that step and change its Weight, such as to 1/3.

Click an answer, then Adjust marks. Marks you set stay when the marking changes.

 Students keep their imported marks until their question has a marking.
