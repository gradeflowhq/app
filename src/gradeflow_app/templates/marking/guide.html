On a text, number or code question, click an answer while editing a marking and choose Full marks, Partial marks or Not allowed. It works with any method, and applies to every student who gave that answer.

The marking keeps its checks and becomes a Workflow, with a check for that answer. Partial marks gives half. To give another share, change that check’s Share, such as to 1/3.

Click an answer, then Adjust marks. Marks you set stay when the marking changes or a new submissions file is imported.

 Students keep their imported marks until their question has a marking.
