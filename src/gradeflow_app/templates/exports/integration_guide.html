In Canvas, open and click New Access Token

    Enter a purpose, click Generate Token and copy the token

    Enter your Canvas address and the token, then click Connect

    In a course, open Canvas and choose its Canvas course

    On an assessment’s Export page, choose its assignment or a new one, then click Send to Canvas
The address and token are kept in this browser. Connect each device you send marks from. Only a course’s editors can link it to Canvas and send marks. No New Access Token button? Your school lets only its Canvas admins make tokens. If Canvas stops accepting the token, connect again with a new one.
